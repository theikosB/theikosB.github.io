<summary>⚙️ Design and Analysis of Algorithms (DAA)</summary>

<details class="material-sub">
<summary>Travelling Salesman Problem</summary>

<div class="material-links">

**Project Description**

The project investigates the Travelling Salesman Problem (TSP), one of the classical NP-hard optimisation problems. It focuses on implementing and comparing exact and approximation algorithms, analysing their computational complexity, and experimentally evaluating their performance on benchmark instances. Particular emphasis is placed on algorithm design, correctness, efficiency, and empirical analysis.

**Submittables**

- 📄 Project Statement
- 📑 Final Report
- 💻 Source Code
- 📊 Experimental Results
- 📽 Presentation Slides

</div>
