Most people would say their bachelor's ended on 5th May 2026 (I'm not that good with dates), or probably end of May, when our results were announced. To me, who had his ideas developed from pop-culture, Bachelor's was the best period of someone's life and initially, I thought I couldn't be more wrong about it. Going into ISI Bangalore (so called tier A), I was amazed to see - nothing.
