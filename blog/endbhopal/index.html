Well I guess this marks the end of my bachelor’s truly, so this moment, the moment when I am having a cake while solo travelling in a train, is special, this moment deserves to be written about.

Most people would say their bachelor’s ended on 5th May 2026 (I’m not that good with dates), or probably end of May, when our results were announced. To me, who had his ideas developed from pop-culture, Bachelor’s was the best period of someone’s life and initially, I thought I couldn’t be more wrong about it. Going into ISI Bangalore (so called tier A), I was amazed to see - nothing. No proper infrastructure, no courts, no swimming pool, no rules, and overall, is still the smallest university camous I’ve been till date.

The Best Phase (Or probably worst?!)
#

It all started when there was 1 week remaining of my departure date - I knew I loved this group of people - bu that was the first time I realised what I meant to the group. Everyone was sad - and no one wanted to show it directly - I heard soo many compliments of me I started doubting their legitimacy - but then I saw their behavior - which was devoting their max time to me - the Mandir walk - the codenames - the politics discussion - the random Valorant plan - the trip to tekri - the stories - the last night game plan but disagreement - the final poker - the room convo or judging till late night - I got to know how much they cared - final closing bonds - the see off - the second reel - the hardest bye.
